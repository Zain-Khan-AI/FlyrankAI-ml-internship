Automating Enterprise Content Audits Using Unsupervised Time-Aware Machine Learning Clustering

📊 Acknowledgments & Data Credit: Built on the FlyRank ML Internship dataset (https://flyrank.ai)

1. Title & Abstract

Abstract: Enterprise search intelligence operations produce massive volumes of daily performance data that are too complex for human content teams to audit manually. This study addresses this challenge by developing an unsupervised K-Means clustering model to automatically categorize pages into behavioral performance groups. Using the FlyRank dataset, we aggregated daily organic metrics and joined them with content structural metadata across two sequential monthly windows. The results successfully isolated four highly distinct and stable content archetypes, which directly translate into automated digital marketing actions. Time-aware validation on next-month unseen data proved that these performance clusters remain positionally and contextually stable, providing a reliable decision-support engine for corporate SEO investments.

2. Introduction / Problem Statement

Large enterprise websites manage tens of thousands of published articles simultaneously. Digital marketing managers and content strategists face a severe operational bottleneck: they cannot efficiently identify which pages are driving revenue, which require minor title tuning, and which are completely dead weight (wasting indexing budget).

This research directly supports executive decision-making by replacing human guesswork with an automated system. By running this model, a Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) or Content Director can instantly audit a database of over 55,000 pages and allocate engineering or copywriting resources exactly where they will generate the highest organic return on investment (ROI).

3. Data Description & Limitations

  • Dataset Release Used: FlyRank Warehouse Release v20260703.
  • Tables Selected: fact_content_daily_performance and dim_content.
  • Date Windows Selected:
    • Feature Window (Training): February 2026 (month=2026-02) — 7,355,108 raw daily rows compressed into 321,546 unique content items.
    • Validation Window (Testing): March 2026 (month=2026-03).
  • Exclusions & Data Cleaning Filters: Long-tail noise filtering was applied by dropping content items with total monthly impressions less than or equal to 100. Pages with missing structural metadata (word_count IS NULL) were excluded from the core distance training matrix.
  • Honest Data Quality Limitation: A severe structural limitation was observed in the raw database: out of 321,546 active pages in February, 241,422 rows (75%) lacked matching metadata in the dimension table. Furthermore, GA4 user engagement metrics (total_scrolls) were heavily sparse with over 50% missing cells, forcing us to drop scroll variables from active feature training to protect model coherence.

4. Methodology

To ensure high-integrity unsupervised boundaries, our model design utilizes the following components:

  • Feature Selection: 5 core continuous variables were generated: total_impressions, total_clicks, avg_position, ctr, and word_count.
  • Mathematical Transformation: Extreme right-skewed metric frequencies (impressions, clicks, word count) were smoothed using log transformation (np.log1p) to eliminate mathematical bias during spatial distance optimization.
  • Feature Scaling: StandardScaler was applied to normalize all coordinates into standardized Z-scores (Mean=0, Std=1) so that large values (word counts) do not overpower decimal bounds (CTR).
  • Baseline Selection: A hard-coded heuristic split based purely on impressions served as the baseline comparison.
  • Validation Split Design: A rigorous time-aware cross-validation split was enforced. The pipeline trains scaling weights and cluster positions entirely on February data, then freezes the hyperparameters, passing March data purely through .transform() and .predict() execution tracks to protect against future leakage.

5. Experimental Results & Performance Comparison

Our K-Means model successfully isolated clean performance profiles that remain highly consistent across sequential historical boundaries.

February 2026 Training Profiles (55,247 Complete Pages)

  • Cluster 0 (Heavy Content / Underdogs): Impressions: 1,452.3 | Clicks: 1.46 | Avg Position: 23.45 | Word Count: 4,347.2 | Count: 10,400 rows.
  • Cluster 1 (Superstars): Impressions: 1,026.4 | Clicks: 17.95 | Avg Position: 7.53 | CTR: 0.0173 (1.73%) | Count: 2,662 rows.
  • Cluster 2 (Zombies): Impressions: 590.3 | Clicks: 0.80 | Avg Position: 8.73 | CTR: 0.0016 | Count: 23,635 rows.
  • Cluster 3 (Hidden Gems): Impressions: 6,080.2 | Clicks: 21.99 | Avg Position: 7.18 | CTR: 0.0038 | Count: 18,550 rows.

March 2026 Unseen Validation Profiles (72,342 Test Pages)

When the frozen model evaluated unseen March matrices, the underlying segment logic reproduced perfectly identical clusters:

  • March Cluster 3 (Superstars Profile): Clicks: 19.51 | Avg Position: 7.91 | CTR: 0.0170 (1.70%). Matches Feb Cluster 1.
  • March Cluster 2 (Traffic Giants Profile): Impressions: 7,321.9 | Clicks: 24.23 | CTR: 0.0037. Matches Feb Cluster 3.
  • March Cluster 0 (Zombies Profile): Impressions: 640.5 | Clicks: 0.83. Matches Feb Cluster 2.
